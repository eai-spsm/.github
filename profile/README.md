# E-AI — Artificial Intelligence Engineering

E-AI is the GitHub organization for the Artificial Intelligence Engineering major (AI Engineering, internally "E-AI") at Satit Prasarnmit Demonstration School, Secondary Division, Bangkok. This org hosts the code, coursework, and projects produced by students in the major.

## About the Major

The Artificial Intelligence Engineering major develops students' knowledge, reasoning, and technical ability across computing, automation, robotics, and artificial intelligence. The curriculum integrates mathematics and science with the design and development of AI systems and software capable of addressing real-world problems, combining theoretical study with applied practice in preparation for higher education.

- **School:** Satit Prasarnmit Demonstration School (Secondary Division)
- **Location:** Sukhumvit 23, Watthana, Bangkok
- **Open for:** Mathayom 4 - 6 (Grades 10 - 12)
- **Cohort size:** roughly 25 students per generation

### Core objectives

- Creative and independent thinking
- Ability to present and explain technical knowledge
- Problem-solving and analytical thinking
- Working understanding of science, engineering, automation, robotics, and AI

## What's in This Org

Repositories here are student and class work from across the major - in-class exercises, competition projects, robotics and computer vision code, research write-ups, and the tools built to document all of it. Individual repositories carry their own README with specifics; this org-level README is just the overview.
